About the App

Acti­vated car­bon and BET

Acti­vated car­bon is a porous, car­bon-rich mate­rial with a large sur­face area, making it useful for adsorp­tion appli­ca­tions such as gas capture and water puri­fi­ca­tion. It can be pro­duced by heat­ing a car­bon-con­tain­ing start­ing mate­rial (a pre­cur­sor) with potas­sium hydrox­ide (KOH) as a chemi­cal acti­vating agent to develop pores.

This app pre­dicts the Brunauer–Emmett–Teller (BET) spe­cific sur­face area (m²/g), which describes the sur­face area per gram of mate­rial esti­mated using the BET gas adsorp­tion method. For acti­vated car­bon, a higher BET sur­face area gen­erally indi­cates more sur­face avail­able for adsorp­tion, making it an impor­tant char­ac­ter­istic for appli­ca­tions such as CO₂ capture and water puri­fi­ca­tion.

Pre­dic­tion inputs

The pre­dic­tion uses six inputs:

Acti­vation tem­per­a­ture (°C)
The tem­per­a­ture at which acti­vation is carried out.
Ratio (dimen­sion­less)
De­fined in this app as 100 di­vided by the pre­cur­sor wt%; for exam­ple, 25 wt% gives a ratio of 4.
KOH activator (wt%)
The mass per­cent­age of the acti­vating agent.
Pre­cur­sor (wt%)
The mass per­cent­age of the start­ing mate­rial.
Heat­ing rate (°C/min)
The rate at which tem­per­a­ture in­creases toward the acti­vation tem­per­a­ture.
Acti­vation time (h)
The dura­tion for which the mate­rial is held at that tem­per­a­ture.

Pre­dic­tion method­ology

The app’s pre­dic­tion model was de­vel­oped by com­par­ing 30 machine learn­ing tech­niques and eval­u­ating their per­for­mance using sta­tis­ti­cal error mea­sures and trend analy­sis. The LMB-based arti­fi­cial neural net­work per­formed best, achiev­ing a cor­re­la­tion co­ef­fi­cient (R) of 0.92 between pre­dicted and experi­mental BET values.

SHAP, a method for ex­plain­ing model predictions, was used to assess how each input influ­enced the re­sults. This analy­sis iden­ti­fied tem­per­a­ture as the most influ­en­tial input, fol­lowed by pre­cur­sor and KOH con­tent, while acti­vation time con­trib­uted the least in the studied data­set. The se­lected model pro­vides rapid esti­mates to help users ex­plore prepa­ra­tion con­di­tions and sup­port experi­mental plan­ning.
